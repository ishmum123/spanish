## Manual QA (2026-09-24, author self-QA, revised after external QA)

- **Source and build.** The 60 passages are hand-written for this pack in
  `tools/passages_src.json`. They are built with the packbuilder at
  vocab-engine 3e1d3b9 plus a Spanish patch, `.cache/pb/es_port.diff`, which
  still has to be ported to vocab-engine. The build writes per-sentence
  `spans`. Command:
  `PYTHONPATH=.cache/pb/tools python3 -m packbuilder passages --lang es .`
- **Builder patch to port.** The patch has three parts.
  (1) In `passages.py`, `Linker.classify` adds two fallbacks for tokens that
  would otherwise count as out of pack. A token inside a pack phrase links
  that phrase, as with the favor of "por favor". A lowercase token whose
  tagger reading is out of pack links the most frequent pack reading of the
  same surface, so leo, vuelve and contenta link leer, volver and contento.
  A sentence-initial PROPN is skipped, because it is a name.
  (2) In `passages.py`, `truecase_openers` lowercases the first word after an
  opening « ¡ ¿ inside a sentence, using the corpus truecase rule. Quoted
  speech such as «Me gusta», «Ahora», «¿Este» and «¡Feliz» was skipped as a
  name and is now linked.
  (3) In `langs/es.py`, `post_resolve` gains `_fue_fuera`. "fuera de",
  "de fuera" and "por fuera" link the adverb fuera instead of ser. A cleft
  such as "lo que aprendí fue a confiar" links ser instead of ir. "fui una
  de ellas", "fue muy/más + adjective" and a passive participle link ser.
  Part 3 also changes corpus sentence links. Porting it needs a Spanish
  rebuild and a review of the sentences.json diff.
